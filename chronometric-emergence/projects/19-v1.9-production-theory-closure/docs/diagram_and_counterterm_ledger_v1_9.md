## Scope

This ledger fixes the generic three-loop 3PI vacuum functional used by the pilot. Species, Lorentz, gauge-group, weak-isospin, color, flavor and contour indices are carried by superfield propagators and vertices. The coefficients below are therefore **generic tensor coefficients**; they are not to be multiplied by ad hoc channel-counting factors in the solver.

The ledger follows

\[
\Gamma_2 = \Gamma_2^{0} + \Gamma_2^{\mathrm{int}},
\]

with the bare/dressed split retained explicitly so that functional derivatives and cancellations are reproducible. The loop count is checked for every topology using

\[
L = I - V + 1.
\]

## Species assignment rules

### Bosonic superfield

\[
\mathcal A = \{A_3^a{}_{\mu}, W^i{}_{\mu}, B_{\mu}, H_A\}.
\]

Retained cubic families: A3A3A3, WWW, WHH, BHH.

Retained renormalized classical quartic families: A3A3A3A3, WWWW, WWHH, BBHH, WBHH, HHHH.

### Grassmann superfield

\[
\Xi = \{c_3^a,c_2^i,Q_L,D_L,D_R\}.
\]

Retained boson-Grassmann vertex families: A3_c3_c3bar, W_c2_c2bar, A3_Q_Qbar, W_Q_Qbar, B_Q_Qbar, A3_D_Dbar, B_D_Dbar, H_Qbar_D, Hdag_Dbar_Q.

The hypercharge ghost is free in the declared gauge and does not appear in interacting skeletons.

## Functional-derivative map

The solver must generate its equations by differentiating the same ledger, not by independently coding self-energies and vertices. In compact notation,

\[
\Sigma_{ab} = 2i\,\frac{\delta \Gamma_2}{\delta G_{ba}},
\qquad
\Pi_{\mu\nu} = 2i\,\frac{\delta \Gamma_2}{\delta D^{\nu\mu}},
\]

with the Grassmann sign convention fixed by the superfield ordering in the implementation. Three-point equations follow from stationarity with respect to the dynamical cubic and boson-Grassmann vertices. Four-point vertices are not independently evolved at three-loop 3PI; their renormalized classical values and split counterterms remain in the functional.

## No-double-counting rules

1. A topology appears exactly once in the generic ledger.
2. Multiplicities arise from explicit index contractions, not duplicate topology rows.
3. Hard/HTL/LPM overlap subtraction is applied at the kernel level and does not create an additional 3PI skeleton.
4. Counterterm diagrams are generated by replacing one declared bare parameter or kernel by its counterterm insertion.
5. The singlet Bethe-Salpeter kernel is generated from the same self-energy functional, \(K=\delta\Sigma_H/\delta G_H\).

## Scope caveat

This ledger freezes the declared three-loop 3PI truncation. It does not prove that a finite non-Abelian 3PI approximation is exactly gauge invariant or renormalizable to all orders. Ward, Slavnov-Taylor, Nielsen, cutoff and conservation residuals remain numerical acceptance gates.
