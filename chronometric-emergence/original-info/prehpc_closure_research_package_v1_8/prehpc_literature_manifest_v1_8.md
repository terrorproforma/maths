# Primary literature used to define the pre-HPC target

1. Ghiglieri and Laine, *Smooth interpolation between thermal Born and LPM rates*, arXiv:2110.07149.
2. Jackson and Laine, *Efficient numerical integration of thermal interaction rates*, arXiv:2107.07132.
3. Bodeker and Schroder, *Equilibration of right-handed electrons*, arXiv:1902.07220.
4. Binosi, *Electroweak pinch technique to all orders*, arXiv:hep-ph/0401182.
5. Gambino and Grassi, *Nielsen identities and the definition of mass*, arXiv:hep-ph/9907254.
6. Berges, *n-Particle irreducible effective action techniques for gauge theories*, arXiv:hep-ph/0401172.
7. Carrington, Frey and Meggison, *The gauge invariance of non-perturbative vertex prescriptions*, arXiv:2606.27213.
8. van Egmond, *Working towards a gauge-invariant description of the Higgs model*, arXiv:2310.06146.
9. Carrington, *Transport coefficients and nPI effective actions*, arXiv:0912.3149.
10. Functional finite-temperature QCD vertex studies including arXiv:1810.12938 and arXiv:2102.13053.

The benchmark code does not silently promote any of these methods into an exact finite-temperature Standard-Model solution. Their role is to define the decomposition, constraints and validation strategy.
