# Spectral Chronometry Literature Search Protocol v0.3

**Search date:** 15 August 2026  
**Purpose:** Falsify broad novelty claims before defining a narrow, defensible contribution.

## Questions searched

1. Has conformal or causal spacetime already been completed by a physical clock scale?
2. Has electroweak/Higgs mass generation already been proposed as the origin of metric scale or physical time?
3. Have Higgs, dilaton, cosmon or Weyl-compensator fields already been tied to atomic standards?
4. Has physical time already been defined as a count of field or wave-function oscillations?
5. Is there an existing theorem stating that a family of clocks defines one local metric exactly when all transition frequencies share one scalar factor?
6. Are differential log-frequency one-forms, a clock-space quotient, or a rank bound on clock-network drift already formulated in this context?
7. Can the Standard Model Higgs alone set electroweak, QCD and gravitational scales while retaining the observed Higgs degree of freedom?

## Sources and inclusion rules

- Primary sources were preferred: original papers, official journal pages and arXiv manuscripts.
- Reviews were used for established atomic-clock sensitivity and varying-constant background.
- Search covered arXiv-indexed web results, journal/search-engine indexing, backward reference checks from key papers, and exact-phrase searches.
- Very recent 2026 preprints were treated as provisional and were not allowed to carry the main novelty case alone.
- A search hit counted as direct prior art only when its mathematical or physical claim overlapped materially, not merely because it used similar vocabulary.

## Main priority findings

- The broad narrative is **not novel**: causal structure determines conformal geometry; clocks fix scale; electroweak/Higgs mass has already been linked to completion of spacetime geometry; oscillation counting as time and Higgs/dilaton clock calibration both exist.
- The Standard Model Higgs alone is not a satisfactory universal chronometric field in ordinary SM+GR.
- No exact indexed match was found for the following combined package:
  1. a local necessary-and-sufficient theorem for compatibility of an entire family of clock spectra;
  2. the quotient of log-frequency space by common Weyl translation;
  3. invariant differential clock-ratio fields as the obstruction to one metric time;
  4. a rank bound relating the number of non-universal scalar directions to clock-network data;
  5. use of that theorem as a Higgs-only versus Higgs-dilaton model-selection test.
- This absence supports only a **candidate novelty statement**. The theorem itself is elementary once stated and may be implicit in universal-coupling or local-position-invariance literature.

## Remaining searches before a formal priority claim

1. Full citation graph for the theorem’s nearest neighbours using Web of Science/Scopus/INSPIRE and MathSciNet where applicable.
2. Specialist searches in metrology, scalar-tensor gravity, Weyl geometry, local position invariance and category/line-bundle formulations.
3. Non-English literature and older pre-arXiv papers.
4. Patent search is probably low-value for the theorem but relevant if a clock-network inference method is claimed as an implementation.
5. Independent review by one expert each in mathematical relativity, Higgs-dilaton cosmology and precision metrology.

## Limitations

- Search-engine absence is not proof of novelty.
- ArXiv timestamps and revisions can be inconsistent with displayed publication dates; version history must be checked before submission.
- Some 2026 items are unrefereed preprints.
- This is a scientific priority audit, not legal advice or a patentability opinion.
- The proposed terminology may be new while the underlying mathematics is implicit or known under another name.
