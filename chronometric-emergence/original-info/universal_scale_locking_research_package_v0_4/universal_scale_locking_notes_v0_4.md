# Universal Scale Locking, Spectral Chronometry, and the Turok-36 Test

**Research notes v0.4 — 16 August 2026**  
**Status:** Candidate model and acceptance protocol; not peer reviewed.

## 1. Executive conclusion

A technically conservative 3+1-dimensional matter model can dynamically generate one order parameter \(\chi\) and lock

\[
\frac{h}{\chi},\qquad
\frac{\Lambda_{\rm QCD}}{\chi},\qquad
\frac{M_{\rm P}}{\chi}
\]

to constants. If every material clock frequency then factorises as

\[
\omega_A(x)=c_A\chi(x),
\]

one operational proper-time metric exists:

\[
\widehat g_{\mu\nu}
=
\left(\frac{\chi}{\chi_*}\right)^2g_{\mu\nu}.
\]

The same exact common scaling makes all Einstein-frame masses constant and removes the scalar's composition-dependent fifth force. Electromagnetic, atomic, molecular and nuclear systems are the readout: they convert the common spectral scale into countable relative phase cycles.

The safe core model uses an ordinary two-derivative hidden \(SU(2)_X\) gauge theory. Turok's 36 dimension-zero fourth-order fields are treated only as a possible ultraviolet replacement for that sector. They are admissible only if their **gauge/Weyl-invariant composite sector**, not merely the elementary \(1/p^4\) variables, passes conventional physical tests.

The construction does **not** yet provide a UV-complete quantum theory of gravity, solve the cosmological constant problem, derive causal order, or predict the observed hierarchy of dimensionless ratios. It co-scales the hierarchy after those ratios are selected.

## 2. Field content

Take

\[
G=G_{\rm SM}\times SU(2)_X,
\]

with:

- the Standard Model Higgs doublet \(H\), with no classical mass term;
- one hidden complex scalar doublet \(\Phi\), charged only under \(SU(2)_X\);
- no hidden chiral fermions in the minimal model;
- the ordinary Standard Model fields;
- Einstein gravity treated as a low-energy effective field theory.

Define gauge-invariant radial fields

\[
h=\sqrt{2H^\dagger H},
\qquad
\chi=\sqrt{2\Phi^\dagger\Phi}.
\]

The Jordan-frame action is

\[
\begin{aligned}
S_J=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\Bigg[&
\frac12F(h,\chi)R
-|D_\mu H|^2-|D_\mu\Phi|^2
-\frac14X^a_{\mu\nu}X^{a\mu\nu}\\
&-V(H,\Phi)
+\mathcal L_{\rm SM}^{m_H^2=0}
+\mathcal L_{\rm grav}^{(4)}
\Bigg],
\end{aligned}
\]

where

\[
F(h,\chi)=\xi_\chi\chi^2+\xi_Hh^2
\]

and

\[
V=
\frac{\lambda_\chi}{2}(\Phi^\dagger\Phi)^2
-\lambda_p(H^\dagger H)(\Phi^\dagger\Phi)
+\frac{\lambda_H}{2}(H^\dagger H)^2.
\]

Curved-space renormalisation requires the usual local counterterms

\[
C_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}^2,
\quad R^2,
\quad E_4,
\quad \Box R,
\]

as well as the non-minimal scalar-curvature couplings. The matter theory is power-counting renormalisable; gravity is explicitly an EFT below its cutoff.

## 3. Generation of the common field

Hidden gauge loops generate a Coleman-Weinberg effective potential. Along the hidden radial direction,

\[
V_X(\chi)=
\frac{\lambda_\chi}{8}\chi^4
+
\frac{9g_X^4}{1024\pi^2}\chi^4
\left[
\ln\left(\frac{g_X^2\chi^2}{4\mu_X^2}\right)-\frac32
\right].
\]

Taking \(\mu_X=g_Xf/2\), the nonzero stationary point obeys

\[
\lambda_\chi(f)=\frac{9g_X^4}{128\pi^2},
\qquad
f\equiv\langle\chi\rangle,
\]

and its radial curvature is

\[
m_{\chi,J}^2
=
\frac{9g_X^4}{128\pi^2}f^2>0.
\]

Thus the scale \(f\) is generated by dimensional transmutation rather than inserted as a classical mass parameter.

## 4. The three locking equations

### 4.1 Electroweak lock

Minimisation in the Higgs direction gives

\[
h^2=\frac{\lambda_p}{\lambda_H}\chi^2
\equiv\zeta_h^2\chi^2.
\]

Hence

\[
\frac{h}{\chi}=\zeta_h=\text{constant}.
\]

The physical Higgs is the angular/alignment excitation nearly orthogonal to the common radial scale direction. With a tiny portal, its mixing with the heavy radial mode is negligible.

### 4.2 Gravitational lock

Along the valley,

\[
F=(\xi_\chi+\xi_H\zeta_h^2)\chi^2
\equiv\xi_{\rm eff}\chi^2.
\]

At the vacuum,

\[
M_{\rm P}^2=\xi_{\rm eff}f^2,
\qquad
\frac{M_{\rm P}}{\chi}=\sqrt{\xi_{\rm eff}}.
\]

### 4.3 QCD lock

Use a physical matching scale proportional to the order parameter,

\[
\mu_*(x)=\kappa\chi(x).
\]

The RG-invariant confinement scale is then

\[
\Lambda_{\rm QCD}(\chi)
=
\kappa\chi
\exp\left[-\int^{g_s(\kappa\chi)}\frac{dg}{\beta_s(g)}\right]
\mathcal T\!\left(\frac{m_q}{\chi},\ldots\right).
\]

If the dimensionless boundary coupling and all threshold ratios are fixed, then

\[
\Lambda_{\rm QCD}=c_Q\chi.
\]

The low-energy local expression is the dilatation Ward coupling

\[
\mathcal L_{\sigma gg}
=
\frac{\sigma}{f}\frac{\beta_s(g_s)}{2g_s}G^a_{\mu\nu}G^{a\mu\nu}
+
\frac{\sigma}{f}\sum_q(1+\gamma_m)m_q\bar q q+\\cdots,
\qquad
\chi=fe^{\sigma/f}.
\]

This is a renormalised local-RG/EFT implementation. A completely explicit all-orders microscopic theory that enforces the common-scale boundary condition across gravity, the hidden sector and QCD remains an open UV task.

### 4.4 Unified lock

\[
\boxed{
\left(
\frac{h}{\chi},
\frac{\Lambda_{\rm QCD}}{\chi},
\frac{M_{\rm P}}{\chi}
\right)
=
\left(\zeta_h,c_Q,\sqrt{\xi_{\rm eff}}\right)
}
\]

The construction locks the ratios; it does not yet calculate why \(\zeta_h\sim10^{-16}\) or why \(c_Q\) has its observed value.

## 5. From scale locking to duration

A material transition frequency has the general form

\[
\omega_A
=
\chi\,
F_A\!\left(
\alpha_{\rm em},y_f,
\frac{\Lambda_{\rm QCD}}{\chi},
\frac{h}{\chi},
\frac{M_{\rm P}}{\chi},\ldots
\right).
\]

On the locked trajectory, every argument is constant, so

\[
\omega_A=c_A\chi.
\]

The clock phase

\[
d\Phi_A=\omega_A ds_g
\]

becomes

\[
d\Phi_A=c_A\chi_*ds_{\widehat g},
\qquad
\widehat g_{\mu\nu}
=
\left(\frac{\chi}{\chi_*}\right)^2g_{\mu\nu}.
\]

Every clock therefore advances at a constant species-specific rate with respect to one metric. The invariant obstruction is the chronometric-shear one-form

\[
\mathcal S_{AB}
=
d\ln\frac{\omega_A}{\omega_B}.
\]

Exact universal chronometry requires

\[
\mathcal S_{AB}=0
\quad\text{for every clock pair.}
\]

This gives the precise physical statement:

> Causal/conformal structure can be primitive, while duration is the common spectral scaling reconstructed from physical phase relations.

## 6. Fifth-force cancellation and clock tests are the same condition

Transform to the Einstein frame,

\[
g^E_{\mu\nu}=\frac{F}{(M_{\rm P}^{(0)})^2}g^J_{\mu\nu}.
\]

A Jordan-frame mass becomes

\[
m_A^E
=
\frac{M_{\rm P}^{(0)}m_A(\chi)}{\sqrt{F(\chi)}}.
\]

On the exact locked trajectory,

\[
m_A=c_A\chi,
\qquad
F=\xi_{\rm eff}\chi^2,
\]

so

\[
m_A^E
=
\frac{c_AM_{\rm P}^{(0)}}{\sqrt{\xi_{\rm eff}}},
\qquad
\alpha_A\equiv\partial_\varphi\ln m_A^E=0.
\]

Thus the common radial mode has no composition-dependent matter coupling in the exact limit. More generally,

\[
\alpha_A-\alpha_B
=
\partial_\varphi\ln\frac{m_A}{m_B}
=
\partial_\varphi\ln\frac{\omega_A}{\omega_B}.
\]

Differential fifth forces and differential clock drift are therefore two probes of the same failure of universal scale locking.

## 9. Lorentzian unitarity and positive spectrum in the safe model

The hidden sector is a conventional gauge theory with two-derivative kinetic terms. Its physical state space is the BRST/gauge-invariant subspace. The relevant radial observable is not a gauge-dependent elementary component but a gauge-invariant composite such as

\[
\mathcal O_\chi
=
\Phi^\dagger\Phi-rac{f^2}{2}.
\]

Its physical two-point function must admit a Källén-Lehmann representation

\[
G_\chi(p^2)
=
\int_0^\infty ds\,
\frac{\rho_\chi(s)}{p^2-s+i0},
\qquad
\rho_\chi(s)\ge0.
\]

The radial pole must have positive residue. Perturbative checks should be supplemented by gauge-invariant lattice correlators and reflection positivity if the sector is pushed into a nonperturbative regime.

## 10. Mapping Turok's 36 fields to the model

Let \(\varphi^I\), \(I=1,\ldots,36\), denote the dimension-zero fourth-order fields. They should not replace \(\chi\) directly. The possible map is

\[
\chi^2
\sim
Z_{\mathcal O}\left|
\left\langle
\frac1{36}G_{IJ}g^{\mu\nu}
\nabla_\mu\varphi^I\nabla_\nu\varphi^J
\right\rangle
\right|
\]

or

\[
\chi=m_{\rm gap}^{\rm FT}.
\]

The required low-energy endpoint is a conventional effective action

\[
\Gamma_{\rm IR}
=
\int\sqrt{-g}\left[
\frac12(\xi_\chi\chi^2+2\xi_HH^\dagger H)R
-\frac12Z_\chi(\partial\chi)^2
-V_{\rm eff}(\chi,H)+\cdots
\right],
\]

with

\[
Z_\chi>0,
\]

a stable nonzero vacuum, and the same three locking equations.

### Spectral obstruction for the elementary fourth-order field

A conventional non-negative Euclidean spectral representation gives

\[
G(Q^2)
=
\int_0^\infty ds\frac{\rho(s)}{Q^2+s}
=
\frac{1}{Q^2}\int\rho(s)ds
-
\frac{1}{Q^4}\int s\rho(s)ds+
O(Q^{-6}).
\]

An exact leading \(1/Q^4\) falloff requires

\[
\int\rho(s)ds=0.
\]

For \(\rho\ge0\), this forces \(\rho=0\). Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{A nontrivial elementary }1/p^4\text{ scalar cannot possess a conventional positive Källén-Lehmann density.}
}
\]

This does not by itself rule out a constrained gauge interpretation, a Krein-space formalism, or a healthy composite IR pole. It means the burden of proof must be carried by the **physical invariant composite sector**.

## 11. Four mandatory Turok-36 tests

### 11.1 Lorentzian unitarity

Specify the physical state space and establish

\[
2\operatorname{Im}\mathcal M_{i\to i}
=
\sum_{n\in\mathcal H_{\rm phys}}
\int d\Pi_n\,|\mathcal M_{i\to n}|^2
\ge0
\]

for amplitudes involving the candidate \(\chi\) channel, Standard Model states and gravity. The proof must survive loops, mixing, the continuum limit and the curved backgrounds used in cosmology.

### 11.2 Positive physical spectral density

Find a gauge/Weyl-invariant \(\mathcal O_\chi\) whose correlator has either a conventional positive spectral measure or an equally strong reconstruction theorem. The infrared pole residue must be positive, and Euclidean invariant correlators should satisfy reflection positivity.

### 11.3 Stable condensate

Demonstrate a regulator-independent, continuum nonzero order parameter with

\[
V'_{\rm eff}(f)=0,
\qquad
V''_{\rm eff}(f)>0,
\qquad
Z_\chi>0,
\]

real dispersion relations, no gradient instability, and a vacuum lifetime longer than the cosmological history being modeled.

### 11.4 Acceptable matter coupling

After coupling to the Standard Model and gravity, calculate:

- the static potential between matter sources;
- the scalar charges \(\alpha_A\);
- clock sensitivities and chronometric shear;
- Higgs-\(\chi\) and graviton-\(\chi\) pole residues.

Require:

\[
\alpha_A-\alpha_B
\]

within equivalence-principle and clock bounds, no linearly confining macroscopic fifth force, and no negative-residue mixed pole.

### Decision rule

- **All four tests pass:** the 36-field singlet may replace the safe hidden \(SU(2)_X\) generator while retaining the same IR locking action.
- **Optical theorem only in an indefinite/Krein space, with no positive invariant spectrum:** do not use it as the physical source of clock scale.
- **Stable condensate but non-universal matter force:** reject or sequester it; it produces clock shear and equivalence-principle violation.
- **No stable positive-residue composite pole:** reject; there is no healthy IR \(\chi\).

## 12. Novelty boundary

The individual ingredients are established: hidden-sector dimensional transmutation, Higgs portals, induced Planck scales, scale-invariant QCD trajectories, fifth-force suppression by universal scaling, and gauge-invariant spectral positivity.

The plausible contribution is the combined selection framework:

1. identify \(h/\chi\), \(\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/\chi\), and \(M_{\rm P}/\chi\) as the three necessary scale locks;
2. prove that the same common factor makes Einstein-frame masses constant and all clock shear vanish;
3. identify differential fifth forces and differential clock drift as the same derivative obstruction;
4. make conventional invariant spectral positivity a hard acceptance test for any proposed microscopic “time field”;
5. separate a healthy infrared construction from the optional speculative 36-field UV completion.

The strongest defensible claim is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{one generated spectral scale}
\Longrightarrow
\{h,\Lambda_{\rm QCD},M_{\rm P},\omega_A\}\propto\chi
\Longrightarrow
\text{one operational proper-time metric}
}
\]

This is a candidate mechanism for the emergence of **duration calibration**, not a derivation of all aspects of time.
