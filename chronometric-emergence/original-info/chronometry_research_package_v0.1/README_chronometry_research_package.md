## Main exact result

For two compact phases with crossed null gradients

\[
k_a=\nabla_a\phi_+,\qquad \ell_a=\nabla_a\phi_-,\qquad
C=-\frac12g^{ab}k_a\ell_b>0,
\]

define

\[
h_{ab}=C g_{ab},\qquad
T=\frac{\phi_++\phi_-}{2},\qquad
R=\frac{\phi_+-\phi_-}{2}.
\]

Then `h` is Weyl invariant and

\[
h^{-1}(dT,dT)=-1,\qquad
h^{-1}(dR,dR)=+1,\qquad
h^{-1}(dT,dR)=0.
\]

The reciprocal rescaling of the two null gradients is exactly a Lorentz boost. Their common rescaling changes the chronometric scale.

## Proposed physical mechanism

A Weyl-weighted order parameter `Sigma` is generated by dimensional transmutation and is coupled through a locking potential

\[
V(C,\Sigma)=\frac{\kappa}{2}(C-\Sigma)^2
+\frac{\beta}{4}\Sigma^2\left(\ln\frac{\Sigma}{\Lambda^2}-\frac12\right)
+\frac{\beta}{8}\Lambda^4.
\]

For positive `kappa` and `beta`, the homogeneous vacuum

\[
C=\Sigma=\Lambda^2
\]

is a strict local minimum. The generated mass gap therefore fixes the conformal/clock scale while leaving rapidity as the ordinary state-dependent boost variable.

The kinematic conformal-completion theorem is exact. The scale-locking action is an effective toy model. A publication-grade theory still requires a microscopic derivation, full perturbative stability analysis, coupling to gravity without circularity, a universality proof for real clock spectra, and a distinct observable prediction.
