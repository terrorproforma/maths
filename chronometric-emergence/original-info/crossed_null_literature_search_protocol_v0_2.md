## Inclusion rule

The priority matrix includes primary papers that either:

- contain an algebraic result used directly by the construction;
- realize the same physical interpretation by different variables;
- provide the closest known dynamical or stability framework; or
- impose a no-go, stability, causality or universality condition on the proposal.

Reviews were used only to locate primary sources and are not relied on for the core priority verdict.
