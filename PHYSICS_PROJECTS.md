## Directory convention for future projects

New transcript-derived investigations should use a distinct root-level folder:

```text
tp-XX-short-project-name/
├── README.md
├── PROJECT_BRIEF.md
├── CITATION.cff
├── paper/
├── code/
├── results/
├── sources/
├── acceptance_matrix.csv
└── manifest_sha256.csv
```

Generated PDFs and binary arrays may be regenerated from committed source where connector limits prevent direct binary import. No absent source file may be silently reconstructed merely to satisfy a manifest.
