# TP-06 — Dark chemistry

**Author:** Angus Muffatti  
**Status:** QUEUED

## Goal

Construct a hidden-sector model whose microphysics can support atoms, molecules, plasma, cooling, dissipative collapse, and complex structure while remaining compatible with cosmology and astrophysics.

## Baseline architecture

\[
G_{\rm total}=G_{\rm SM}\times G_D,
\]

with explicitly declared dark matter fields, mediators, stable charges, portals, and thermal history.

## Required outputs

- bound-state spectrum and recombination;
- molecular or many-body channels;
- cooling functions and phase diagram;
- self-interaction and halo constraints;
- relic abundance and dark-radiation history;
- direct, collider, stellar, and cosmological portal constraints;
- at least one discriminating observable.

## Terminal outcome

A quantitative map separating collisionless dark matter, self-interacting matter, dark plasma, dark atoms, and genuine dark chemistry.
