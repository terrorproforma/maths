S-01 & Quantum gravity / ultraviolet completion & ULTRAVIOLET INCOMPLETENESS & Pure Einstein gravity has a nonzero two-loop divergence as a finite-parameter perturbative QFT, while quantum GR is predictive as a Wilsonian EFT at low energy. This is not evidence that classical GR or low-energy gravitational EFT is internally inconsistent. \\
S-02 & Vacuum energy / cosmological constant & NATURALNESS AND PARAMETER ORIGIN & A small positive effective cosmological constant fits the background expansion. Its radiative stability and relation to quantum vacuum contributions are unexplained. A fitted Lambda is not an observational failure of GR; the seam is explanatory and radiative. \\
S-03 & Classical singularities and geodesic incompleteness & INACCESSIBLE REGIME / CLASSICAL BREAKDOWN & Singularity theorems establish causal geodesic incompleteness under specified focusing, causal and global assumptions. They do not identify a physical point of infinite density or a unique completion. Not every GR solution is singular and observations of black-hole exteriors do not directly observe an interior singularity. \\
S-04 & Dark matter identity & EMPIRICAL MISSING COMPONENT & Multiple cosmological and astrophysical datasets require additional gravitating structure in the standard framework, but no microscopic dark-matter particle has been confirmed. A null WIMP search is not evidence against all dark matter, and modified-gravity alternatives must reproduce the same multi-scale evidence. \\
S-05 & Dark energy microphysics and possible evolution & SUCCESSFUL PARAMETRIZATION, ORIGIN OPEN & A cosmological constant gives a successful minimal fit. DESI DR2 combinations show a dataset- and parametrization-dependent preference for evolving w(a), not a settled detection. The absence of a microphysical explanation is not an internal contradiction of LambdaCDM. \\
S-06 & Neutrino masses and lepton-number structure & EMPIRICAL EXTENSION TO MINIMAL RENORMALIZABLE SM & Oscillations require nonzero mass splittings. The renormalizable SM field content gives massless neutrinos; the dimension-five operator is the minimal data-required EFT extension. The data do not yet determine Dirac versus Majorana mass, absolute ordering in all respects, or the UV mediator. \\
S-07 & Baryogenesis / matter-antimatter asymmetry & MISSING DYNAMICAL HISTORY & The observed baryon asymmetry is not generated in an accepted calculation by the minimal SM with its measured Higgs and standard thermal history. Sphalerons provide B+L violation but the electroweak transition is a crossover and SM CP violation is insufficient in standard scenarios. This is not a mathematical inconsistency; a primordial asymmetric boundary condition can be parametrized but is not explained. \\
S-08 & Inflation or an alternative origin of primordial perturbations & MISSING INITIAL-CONDITION DYNAMICS & A nearly adiabatic, nearly scale-invariant scalar spectrum is well measured. The generating field content, initial quantum state and reheating history are not uniquely selected. Inflation is a broad mechanism class, not a single verified Lagrangian; alternatives are not excluded merely by being alternatives. \\
S-09 & Electroweak hierarchy / Higgs sensitivity & NATURALNESS & The measured electroweak scale is radiatively sensitive to heavy thresholds in many UV completions. The SM as a renormalized QFT is internally consistent without a naturalness postulate. Quadratic sensitivity is not by itself an observed anomaly or a regulator-independent proof of new particles near the weak scale. \\
S-10 & Strong CP & UNEXPLAINED SMALL PARAMETER & QCD permits CP violation, yet neutron-EDM limits require an extremely small effective angle. The smallness is unexplained in the SM. A null EDM is not an inconsistency; it is a severe parameter-selection problem. \\
S-11 & Flavour, Yukawa and mixing structure & UNEXPLAINED PARAMETERS & Masses, mixing angles and CP phases are measured inputs. Their hierarchies and textures are not predicted by the SM gauge principle. A fitted parameter is not an inconsistency, and a conjunction of texture assumptions is not automatically an explanation. \\
S-12 & Why three generations? & UNEXPLAINED DISCRETE CHOICE & Anomaly cancellation occurs generation by generation and therefore does not fix N\_g=3 in the minimal four-dimensional SM. The repeated spectrum is not evidence for any particular algebraic or geometric model. \\
S-13 & Cosmological initial state and thermodynamic arrow & MISSING INITIAL/BOUNDARY CONDITION & Field equations require a state and constrained initial data. The observed low-entropy past and perturbation state are not fixed by the GR+SM action. Deterministic equations do not eliminate the need for boundary data. \\
S-14 & Black-hole information and semiclassical unitarity & SEMICLASSICAL/UV TENSION & Semiclassical reasoning creates a tension between horizon thermality, complete evaporation and unitary quantum evolution. Observations probe exteriors, not the full quantum information map. No experiment has established nonunitarity, and the paradox is not a theorem that every candidate resolution fails. \\
S-15 & Global form of the Standard Model gauge group & UNRESOLVED GLOBAL STRUCTURE & The known local spectrum fixes the Lie algebra but not uniquely the faithful global gauge group; differences occur in line operators, monopoles and allowed heavy charges. This does not change ordinary perturbative SM predictions at accessible energies. \\
S-16 & Electroweak-vacuum longevity & PARAMETER-SENSITIVE EXTRAPOLATION & High-scale extrapolation often places the SM near a metastability boundary; the conclusion depends on top-mass interpretation, alpha\_s, matching and higher-dimensional operators. A metastable vacuum with lifetime longer than cosmological history is not inconsistent. \\
S-17 & Accidental baryon/lepton symmetries and proton stability & UV-SENSITIVE ACCIDENTAL STRUCTURE & Renormalizable SM interactions conserve baryon and lepton number perturbatively (with electroweak anomaly qualifications), while generic higher-dimensional operators need suppression. Observed proton longevity does not prove exact baryon number. \\
S-18 & Active precision-anomaly watchlist & VOLATILE EMPIRICAL TENSIONS & H0, muon g-2, the CDF II W mass, selected CKM/flavour quantities and DESI dark-energy fits deserve continued scrutiny. Their status is not uniform and changes with data/theory updates. No watchlist entry is a universal fatal gate for every successor theory; fitting one at the cost of established domains is failure. \\
