# Minimum viable successor - one-page checklist

A theory claiming to supersede GR + SM is **not minimally viable** until every applicable fatal item is demonstrated, not merely asserted.

## Definition and algebra

- [ ] Theory tuple is complete: space, fields, symmetries, dynamics, observables, state/initial/boundary data.
- [ ] Gauge/diffeomorphism redundancies are separated from physical degrees of freedom.
- [ ] Local anomaly polynomial vanishes or a complete cancellation mechanism is supplied.
- [ ] Global anomalies and the faithful global gauge group are checked.
- [ ] Constraint algebra closes and constraints propagate.

## Dynamics

- [ ] Quadratic spectrum has no ghost, tachyon or gradient instability in every claimed background.
- [ ] Initial-value problem is strongly hyperbolic or otherwise proven well posed.
- [ ] Physical amplitudes/correlators are unitary or have positive physical spectral density.
- [ ] Characteristic cones and causal prescription are consistent.
- [ ] Loops generate only operators included in the declared EFT, with cutoff and error estimate.

## Recovery

- [ ] Einstein equations emerge in the low-curvature classical limit.
- [ ] Two tensor graviton helicities and observed SM chiral representations emerge at low energy.
- [ ] Measured particle poles, widths, Higgs/EW precision, CKM/PMNS and QCD are recovered.
- [ ] Lorentz/CPT and equivalence-principle bounds are passed.
- [ ] Solar-system, pulsar, black-hole exterior and GW limits are passed.
- [ ] BBN, recombination, CMB, BAO/SNe/growth and late acceleration are reproduced.

## Evidence discipline

- [ ] Every claimed pass has an equation, executable test, input version and uncertainty/domain.
- [ ] Established results, transcript-motivated claims, inferences and new conjectures are labelled separately.
- [ ] Active anomalies are frozen to explicit data/theory versions and are not treated as universal facts.
- [ ] A fatal failure is not averaged away by successes elsewhere.
- [ ] Novelty is audited separately and is never a substitute for correctness.

**Kill rule:** any applicable fatal gate scored `0` rejects the candidate in its claimed domain. There is no compensating total score.
