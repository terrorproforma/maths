# TP-02 project brief — Independent reconstruction of Geometric Unity

## Mission

Produce a source-faithful, independently checkable axiomatization of Geometric Unity from its primary materials and test it against TP-00. Do not reconstruct the theory from podcast slogans, secondary commentary or sympathetic interpolation.

## Terminal outcomes

The project must end in one of three states:

1. a complete typed theory that passes the relevant algebraic, perturbative and recovery gates;
2. a precise negative result identifying a fatal inconsistency or failed recovery limit; or
3. a source-incompleteness theorem identifying exactly which indispensable definitions/equations are absent and why no unique reconstruction is possible.

## Required sequence

1. Freeze the primary corpus and preserve source terminology.
2. Extract the complete tuple
   \[
   \mathfrak G=(X,Y,\text{bundles},\text{fields},\text{symmetries},S,\mathcal O,\mathcal I,\mathcal B).
   \]
3. Resolve notation by reversible aliases only.
4. Re-derive the inhomogeneous gauge group, its actions and the augmented torsion covariance.
5. Type the Shiab family and choose each model branch explicitly.
6. Independently vary the bosonic and fermionic actions.
7. Construct the deformation/BRST complex and test nilpotency or closure.
8. Linearize on every claimed background; compute kinetic matrices, principal symbols and degrees of freedom.
9. Prove the stated representation branching and observed-field decomposition rather than merely matching dimensions.
10. Derive the four-dimensional low-energy gauge group, chirality, family structure, interactions and gravity limit.
11. Compute local/global anomalies, pole spectrum, unitarity, causality and EFT closure.
12. Only after those pass, confront laboratory, astrophysical and cosmological constraints.

## Governing acceptance rule

Use the frozen TP-00 YAML. Scores are independent. An applicable fatal gate at zero rejects the claimed domain; no aggregate score is permitted.
