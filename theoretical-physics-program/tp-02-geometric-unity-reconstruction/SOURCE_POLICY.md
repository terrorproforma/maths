# Source policy

## Primary corpus

The source-faithful reconstruction begins only from materials published on the official Geometric Unity site:

1. Eric Weinstein, *Geometric Unity: Author's Working Draft*, version 1.0, 1 April 2021, 69 pages.
2. The official transcript and supporting material for the 2013 Oxford lecture, republished on the official site.
3. The official Geometric Unity landing page, used only for provenance and version linkage.

## Rights boundary

The draft contains an explicit copyright and derivative-use notice. This repository does not mirror the PDF or reproduce long passages. It stores:

- bibliographic and version metadata;
- page, section and equation references;
- minimal definitions needed for criticism and verification;
- independent paraphrases and mathematical derivations;
- executable checks written for this project.

## Evidence roles

- **Primary source:** establishes what the proposal says.
- **Independent derivation:** establishes what follows from a typed interpretation.
- **External primary literature:** establishes mathematical precedent, experimental bounds or counterexamples.
- **Inference:** explicitly labelled conclusion joining the above.

No secondary criticism is used to fill an undefined object. When the primary source permits multiple interpretations, the reconstruction branches and records each branch.

## Source-preservation rule

Terminology such as `Observerse`, `Chimeric Bundle`, `Zorro`, `Shiab`, `augmented torsion`, `swervature` and `imposter generation` is preserved as source terminology. Project aliases are mechanical and reversible; they are not silent corrections.
