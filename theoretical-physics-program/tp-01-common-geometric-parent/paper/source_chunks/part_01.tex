Keeping $c_3$ explicit prevents a representation-dependent trace convention from masquerading as a physical coefficient prediction.

\section{The exact action genealogy inherited from v1.0}
\label{sec:genealogy}

\subsection{Relative Chern--Weil parent and transgression}

Let $X_6=M_5\times I_t$, let $A$ and $\bar A$ be endpoint connections on one declared principal bundle, and define
\[
\Theta=A-\bar A,
\qquad
A_t=\bar A+t\Theta,
\qquad
F_t=\bar F+t\bar D\Theta+t^2\Theta^2.
\]
Choose the six-dimensional interpolating connection with no $\dd t$ component,
\[
\cA(x,t)=A_t(x),
\qquad
\cF=F_t+\dd t\,\Theta.
\]
The relative characteristic functional is
\[
S_{\rm CW}[\cA]=\kappa_6\int_{X_6}P_3(\cF,\cF,\cF).
\]
Since $F_t^3$ is a horizontal six-form on a five-dimensional base and $(\dd t)^2=0$,
\[
P_3(\cF^3)=3\dd t\,P_3(\Theta,F_t,F_t).
\]
Therefore
\[
S_{\rm CW}=k\int_{M_5}T_5(A,\bar A),
\qquad
T_5(A,\bar A)=3\int_0^1P_3(\Theta,F_t,F_t)\dd t,
\]
with
\[
\dd T_5=P_3(F^3)-P_3(\bar F^3).
\]
This is an exact off-shell fibre-integration identity in Chern--Weil theory, not a dynamical equivalence claim \citep{ChernSimons1974,MoraOleaTroncosoZanelli2006}.

Locally,
\[
T_5(A,\bar A)=\CS_5(A)-\CS_5(\bar A)-\dd B_4(A,\bar A).
\]
The relative form is strictly invariant under simultaneous finite gauge transformations of the endpoints.  The exact boundary form is part of the action and later supplies the differentiable surface generator; it is not optional decoration \citep{MoraOleaTroncosoZanelli2006,PaisSalgadoRebolledoVera2023}.

\subsection{Five-dimensional Chern--Simons daughter}

On a closed $M_5$, or with $B_4$ retained, set $\bar A=0$.  Then
\[
T_5(A,0)=\CS_5(A),
\qquad
\dd\CS_5(A)=P_3(F^3).
\]
For the anti-de Sitter split
\[
A=\frac12\omega^{\widetilde a\widetilde b}J_{\widetilde a\widetilde b}
+\ell_5^{-1}E^{\widetilde a}J_{\widetilde a5},
\qquad \widetilde a=0,\ldots,4,
\]
the action is the tuned Lovelock combination
\[
\begin{aligned}
S_{\CS,5}=C_5\int\epsilon_{\widetilde a\widetilde b\widetilde c\widetilde d\widetilde e}
\Big(&R^{\widetilde a\widetilde b}R^{\widetilde c\widetilde d}E^{\widetilde e}
+\frac{2}{3\ell_5^2}R^{\widetilde a\widetilde b}E^{\widetilde c}E^{\widetilde d}E^{\widetilde e}\\
&+\frac{1}{5\ell_5^4}E^{\widetilde a}E^{\widetilde b}E^{\widetilde c}E^{\widetilde d}E^{\widetilde e}\Big),
\end{aligned}
\]
with coefficient ratios $1:2/3:1/5$ fixed by the homotopy integral.  Five-dimensional Chern--Simons gravity is not topological in the three-dimensional sense: generic sectors carry local modes \citep{Chamseddine1989,BanadosGarayHenneaux1995,BanadosGarayHenneaux1996}.

\subsection{Exact circle reduction and the Einstein sector}

\qquad
A=\bar A+\Phi^A(x)J_{A4}\,\dd y,

where $A,B\in\{0,1,2,3,5\}$.  Since $(\dd y)^2=0$,
\[

=\beta\int_{M_4}\epsilon_{ABCDE}\Phi^AF^{BC}F^{DE},
\qquad
\beta=3kc_3L_y.
\]
This reduction is exact off shell within the ansatz.  If $\Phi$ is varied, its equation includes
\[
\epsilon_{ABCDE}F^{BC}\wedge F^{DE}=0.
\]
If instead one imposes external fixed holonomy
\[

\qquad

MacDowell--Mansouri and covariant compensator formulations are established mechanisms; the question here is whether this fixed sector is actually generated as a regular parent reduction \citep{MacDowellMansouri1977,StelleWest1980,Chamseddine1990}.

\section{Dirac analysis of the five-dimensional operational parent}
\label{sec:dirac}

\subsection{Primary constraints and presymplectic matrix}

Split $M_5=\mathbb R\times\Sigma_4$, with spatial indices $I,J=1,\ldots,4$, and let $a=1,\ldots,N$ label the gauge algebra.  The Chern--Simons action has first-order form
\[
I=\int\dd t\int_{\Sigma_4}
\left[\ell_a^I(A)\dot A_I^a-A_0^aK_a\right].
\]
For the invariant tensor $g_{abc}$,
\[
K_a=-g_{abc}\epsilon^{IJKL}F^b_{IJ}F^c_{KL},
\]
and the primary constraints are
\[
\phi_a^I=p_a^I-\ell_a^I(A)\approx0.
\]
Their Poisson matrix is
\[
\Om_{ab}^{IJ}
=\{\phi_a^I,\phi_b^J\}
=-4\epsilon^{IJKL}g_{abc}F^c_{KL}.
\]
The improved Gauss generator
\[
G_a=K_a-D_I\phi_a^I
\]
obeys, weakly,
\[
\{G_a,G_b\}=f^c{}_{ab}G_c,
\qquad
\{\phi_a^I,G_b\}=f^c{}_{ab}\phi_c^I.
\]
The identity
\[
\Om_{ab}^{IJ}F^b_{KJ}=\delta^I{}_K K_a
\]
provides four null vectors on the constraint surface.  The combinations
\[
H_K=F^a_{KJ}\phi_a^J
\]
generate improved spatial diffeomorphisms \citep{BanadosGarayHenneaux1995,BanadosGarayHenneaux1996}.

A \emph{generic} sector has $\operatorname{rank}\Om=4N-4$.  A \emph{regular} sector has independent constraint gradients, $\operatorname{rank}(\partial K_a/\partial F)=N$.  In a canonical sector both conditions hold, so for $N=15$ the split is:

The local configuration-space degree count is
\[
\dof
=\frac12\left[8N-2(N+4)-(4N-4)\right]

\]
The conjugate momenta of $A_0$ and their gauge-fixing partners do not change this count.  This reproduces the established Hamiltonian result for higher-dimensional Chern--Simons theories \citep{BanadosGarayHenneaux1995,BanadosGarayHenneaux1996}.
