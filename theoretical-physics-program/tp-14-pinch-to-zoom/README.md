# TP-14 — Pinch-to-zoom

**Author:** Angus Muffatti  
**Status:** QUEUED

## Goal

Define an invariant, dynamically generated modification of physical separation or travel time and determine whether it can exist without being a coordinate rescaling, a conformal relabelling, an unstable stress tensor, or a violation of causal consistency.

## Candidate mathematical translations

The investigation must distinguish at least:

1. conformal deformation
   \[
   \widetilde g_{\mu\nu}=\Omega^2(x)g_{\mu\nu};
   \]
2. disformal deformation
   \[
   \widetilde g_{\mu\nu}=A(\phi,X)g_{\mu\nu}+B(\phi,X)\nabla_\mu\phi\nabla_\nu\phi;
   \]
3. Weyl-geometric scale transport;
4. dynamical warp or wormhole geometries;
5. mere coordinate transformations, which are not physical propulsion mechanisms.

## Required outputs

- a precise invariant observable measuring the proposed distance or travel-time change;
- a complete action or equivalent dynamical law;
- field equations, constraints, energy budget and boundary conditions;
- principal symbol, characteristic cones and stability analysis;
- an exact solution or a restricted no-go theorem;
- recovery of the ordinary GR limit;
- a dimensionless discriminator not reducible to coordinates.
