# TP-03 branch ledger

| Branch | Scalar content/convention | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PS0` | Complex \((1,2,2)\) plus \((10,1,3)\) | **REJECTED** | Tree-level relations \(M_d=M_e\) and \(M_u=M_D^\nu\). |
| `PS1` | `PS0` plus complex \((15,2,2)\), gauge symmetry only | **UNDERDETERMINED** | The general scalar potential has 7 quadratic and 131 quartic real parameters. |
| `PS1-MM` | `PS1` with the declared moment-map high-scale potential | **PHASE 2B PASS** | Bounded global SM-preserving vacuum, 9 Goldstones, no physical scalar tachyon. |
| `PS1-EW` | `PS1-MM` plus a fully declared bidoublet mass/mixing and low-energy matching branch | **NEXT** | Must produce one light Higgs doublet, fermion fits, thresholds and flavour/proton constraints. |
| `PS1P` | Parity-completed branch including a left triplet | **DEFERRED** | Different vacuum, type-II seesaw and domain-wall structure. |
| `PS-D` | Doublet-breaking alternative | **DEFERRED** | Different neutrino and topological-defect structure. |

No result from one branch is silently transferred to another.
