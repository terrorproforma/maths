# TP-03 project brief — Complete Pati–Salam benchmark

## Mission

Construct a fully declared, anomaly-free and symmetry-broken benchmark based on

\[
SU(4)_C\times SU(2)_L\times SU(2)_R,
\]

then test it against the frozen TP-00 gates without aggregating fatal failures.

## Terminal outcomes

1. a reproducible viable benchmark with a complete scalar vacuum, fermion/neutrino fit, threshold
   running and discriminating observables;
2. a restricted no-go theorem for a clearly declared minimal class; or
3. a branch split identifying exactly which additional symmetry or field is required.

## Completed

- Phase 1: anomaly and hypercharge audit; `PS0` Yukawa obstruction; \((15,2,2)\) repair.
- Phase 2A: neutral \((10,1,3)\) breaking orbit and heavy-vector spectrum.
- Phase 2B: complete renormalisable invariant count and 138-channel coupling basis for complex `PS1`; certified `PS1-MM`
  high-scale vacuum and scalar Hessian.

## Current phase

`PS1-EW`: bidoublet mass mixing, one-light-Higgs matching, RG evolution, fermion/neutrino fit,
flavour and baryon-violation audit.
