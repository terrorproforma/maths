# TP-07 — Dark light

**Author:** Angus Muffatti  
**Status:** QUEUED

## Goal

Turn the phrase “dark light” into a precise, falsifiable theory object and distinguish it from an ordinary dark photon, generic dark radiation, and composite hidden-sector emission.

## Candidate interpretations

1. a massless gauge boson of an unbroken \(U(1)_D\);
2. a very light boson from a weakly broken gauge group;
3. composite radiation from a confining hidden sector;
4. a decoupled radiation bath with its own thermal history.

## Required outputs

- exact gauge group and field content;
- mass-generation or exact-masslessness theorem;
- thermal population and \(\Delta N_{\rm eff}\);
- interactions with dark matter;
- portal and detector response;
- stellar, cosmological, laboratory, and structure constraints;
- observable criterion making “dark light exists” unambiguous.
