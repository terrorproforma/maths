# Known limits and recovery maps

This file turns the phrase "recover GR and the Standard Model" into operational limits. Limits must commute where a candidate claims they do; noncommuting limits must be identified.

## 1. Gravity

### Classical limit

Take quantum occupation/actions large compared with `hbar`, or equivalently suppress loops while holding renormalized couplings fixed. The effective equations must reduce to

\[
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=\bar M_P^{-2}T_{\mu\nu}+O(E^2/\Lambda_{\rm UV}^2).
\]

### Weak-field and Newtonian limit

Set `g_00=-(1+2 Phi)`, `g_ij=(1-2 Psi)delta_ij`, with `|Phi|,|Psi| << 1` and `v << 1`. For nonrelativistic, weakly stressed matter,

\[
\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi G\rho,
\]

up to corrections below the applicable inverse-square, equivalence-principle and PPN limits.

### Local inertial limit

At every regular event and over a region small relative to curvature radii, matter propagation must reduce to special-relativistic local physics, with universal metric coupling to the observed precision.

### Post-Newtonian and radiation limits

The candidate must reproduce the PPN coefficients, post-Keplerian pulsar observables and the quadrupolar tensor radiation limit. Extra scalar/vector radiation must decouple or remain below timing and GW bounds.

### Black-hole exterior limit

For masses and spins probed by current data, stable exterior solutions and perturbations must reproduce Kerr/GR observables to the catalog and imaging precision. A different interior is allowed only if it does not spoil the exterior or conservation laws.

## 2. Standard Model and QFT

### Low-energy chiral gauge limit

Below the new-physics scale, the light fields must transform as three generations of

\[
Q_L:(3,2)_{1/6},\ u_R:(3,1)_{2/3},\ d_R:(3,1)_{-1/3},\ L_L:(1,2)_{-1/2},\ e_R:(1,1)_{-1},
\]

plus `H:(1,2)_{1/2}`, with `Q=T3+Y`, correct chirality and anomaly cancellation.

### Electroweak broken phase

For `H=(0,(v+h)/sqrt(2))`, derive

\[
m_W=gv/2,\qquad m_Z=v\sqrt{g^2+g'^2}/2,\qquad m_f=y_fv/\sqrt2,
\]

and match poles, widths, CKM/PMNS structure and Higgs signal strengths in a declared renormalization scheme.

### Fermi limit

For momentum transfer `Q << m_W`, charged-current amplitudes must reduce to local four-fermion interactions with

\[
G_F/\sqrt2=g^2/(8m_W^2)
\]

plus controlled radiative and higher-dimensional corrections.

### QED limit

At energies below weak thresholds and outside nonperturbative QCD, recover a massless photon, conserved electric charge, Ward identities, the measured running of alpha and the observed charged spectrum.

### QCD limits

- `Q >> Lambda_QCD`: asymptotic freedom, factorization and perturbative running.
- `Q ~ Lambda_QCD`: confinement, chiral symmetry breaking, hadron spectrum and lattice thermodynamics.
- Heavy-quark limits: decoupling and HQET relations.

### Neutrino limit

The renormalizable SM limit has massless neutrinos; the data-compatible frontier baseline includes either the Weinberg operator or an explicitly matched Dirac/Majorana extension. Oscillation probabilities, nonunitarity and beta-decay observables must be matched.

### Decoupling limit

For a heavy mass `M -> infinity` generated independently of electroweak breaking, low-energy amplitudes must admit an Appelquist-Carazzone/Wilsonian expansion unless the theory explicitly demonstrates nondecoupling and its symmetry origin.

## 3. Cosmology

### FLRW background limit

For homogeneous/isotropic data, recover a Friedmann solution with the measured radiation, baryon, dark-sector/effective-gravity and late-acceleration history.

### Thermal history

A successor must pass through successful neutrino decoupling, BBN, recombination and matter domination. Extra species, entropy injection and varying couplings must be evolved, not hand-waved away.

### Linear perturbation limit

Derive gauge-invariant scalar/vector/tensor perturbation equations and match CMB, BAO, lensing and growth. Initial conditions and quantum state are inputs unless the theory derives them.

### LambdaCDM limit

A candidate may replace dark matter, dark energy or inflation, but must contain a parameter region or controlled effective regime that reproduces the empirical success of base LambdaCDM where it succeeds.

## 4. Order-of-limits warnings

Check explicitly whether the following commute:

- massless and infinite-volume limits;
- zero-frequency and zero-momentum response limits;
- classical and long-time limits;
- continuum and thermodynamic limits;
- decoupling and symmetry-restoration limits;
- weak-coupling and late-time thermalization limits.

Failure to commute is not automatically fatal, but it must be declared and tied to an observable prescription.
