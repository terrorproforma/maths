# TP-12 — Physics before the spacetime manifold

**Author:** Angus Muffatti  
**Status:** QUEUED

## Goal

Choose one non-manifold primitive structure and prove a controlled reconstruction of Lorentzian continuum geometry and an Einstein-like low-energy action.

## Candidate primitives

- causal order;
- operator or noncommutative algebra;
- category of processes;
- graph, spin network, incidence system, twistor space, or quantum code.

## Required reconstruction

\[
\mathfrak X\longrightarrow(M,[g])\longrightarrow(M,g).
\]

The first map must recover dimension, topology, differentiability, locality, Lorentzian signature, and causal structure. The second must recover physical scale.

## Terminal outcome

A genuine recovery theorem with convergence/error control and observable content, or a precise obstruction showing which manifold property cannot follow from the declared primitive.
