# TP-09 — Consistent spin-3/2 matter

**Author:** Angus Muffatti  
**Status:** QUEUED

## Goal

Construct one interacting causal and unitary spin-\(3/2\) matter model, or establish a sharp consistency classification/no-go result for the declared local field-theory class.

## Core field

\[
\psi_\mu
\]

with a Rarita–Schwinger-type free limit and an explicit mechanism eliminating unphysical spin-\(1/2\) components.

## Required outputs

- physical degree-of-freedom count;
- constraint and gauge structure;
- causal characteristics in backgrounds and interactions;
- positive-norm physical spectrum;
- anomaly and radiative consistency;
- elementary, composite, confined, or gravitino-like interpretation;
- stability, production, and detection channels.

High-spin consistency gates are evaluated before phenomenology.
