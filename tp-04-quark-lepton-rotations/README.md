# TP-04 — Quark–lepton rotations and matter stability

**Author:** Angus Muffatti  
**Status:** QUEUED  
**Preferred dependency:** consume TP-03's explicit unified spectrum when available.

## Goal

Identify the broken unified generators that connect quarks and leptons, integrate out their mediators, derive the complete low-energy operator basis, and calculate the actual proton, neutron, nuclear, and meson transition bounds.

## Required outputs

- adjoint decomposition under the Standard Model subgroup;
- vector/scalar leptoquark generator catalogue;
- changes in \(B\), \(L\), \(B-L\), and electric charge;
- Wilson coefficients and renormalisation-group evolution;
- matter-stability lifetimes and experimental comparison;
- exact symmetry conditions that forbid or suppress dangerous operators.

## Terminal outcome

A theorem/operator catalogue showing which bulk-matter conversion processes are allowed, forbidden, or negligible in each declared model class.
