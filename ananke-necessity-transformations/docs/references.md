# Technical references

1. Michael M. Wolf and David Pérez-García, **Assessing dimensions from evolution**,
   *Physical Review Letters* 102, 190504 (2009). Preprint:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.2542>
2. Stephen Kirkland, Thomas Laffey and Helena Šmigoc, **The Karpelevič Region
   Revisited**, *Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications* 490, 124249
   (2020). Preprint: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.02452>
3. Marco Fanizza et al., **Quantum theory in finite dimension cannot explain every
   finite-dimensional process**, preprint: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.11225>
4. Qingqing Huang, Rong Ge, Sham Kakade and Munther Dahleh, **Minimal Realization
   Problems for Hidden Markov Models**, Allerton Conference (2014). Preprint:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.3698>
5. Devon N. Munger, Andrew L. Nickerson and Pietro Paparella, **Demystifying the
   Karpelevič theorem**, preprint: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03849>

These references establish much of the mathematical machinery. ANANKE's proposed
contribution is the adversarial, finite-data integration and the ontology-cost/design
interpretation, not ownership of the underlying realization or stochastic-spectrum
theorems.
