# TP-11 — Singularity resolution

**Author:** Angus Muffatti  
**Status:** QUEUED

## Goal

Test one concrete cosmological or black-hole singularity-resolution mechanism using geodesic completeness, invariant observables, well-posed evolution, perturbative stability, and recovery of ordinary General Relativity outside the high-curvature regime.

## Required outputs

- precise replacement action or dynamical law;
- explicit nonsingular background solution;
- causal-geodesic extension analysis;
- bounded physical invariants, not merely coordinate regularity;
- full perturbation equations and stability;
- constraint propagation and entropy/information accounting;
- compact-object exterior and cosmological recovery;
- falsifiable observational discriminator.

Cosmological initial singularities, black-hole interiors, Cauchy horizons, and topology change are treated separately.
