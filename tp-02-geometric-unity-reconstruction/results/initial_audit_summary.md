# TP-02 Phase 1 initial audit summary

## Verdict

\[
\boxed{
\text{Primary-source reconstruction is feasible, but Geometric Unity is not yet a complete independently typed theory.}
}
\]

## What has been completed

- Official primary corpus frozen to the 2021 working draft and official Oxford lecture transcript.
- Rights-aware source policy established; the copyrighted draft is cited rather than mirrored.
- Twenty-seven source objects indexed by section/equation and reconstruction status.
- Initial theory tuple typed through spaces, bundles, principal bundle, field spaces, inhomogeneous gauge group, distinguished connection, augmented torsion and bosonic action proposals.
- Mechanical notation aliases frozen.
- Seventeen major source claims separated from project checks.
- Nineteen TP-00 gates scored independently at the current evidence level.
- Twelve exact dimension checks implemented and passed.

## Strongest positive result

The dimension architecture is internally coherent at the arithmetic level:

\[
\dim Y=4+10=14,
\quad
\dim S_{7,7}^{+}=64,
\quad
\dim RS_{7,7}^{+}=832,
\]

and

\[
832=64+192+576.
\]

This confirms that the prominent representation dimensions in the draft are not simple arithmetic accidents.

## What this does not establish

It does not prove:

- the Chimeric Bundle signature globally;
- the Clifford embedding or representation branching;
- the gauge covariance of a selected Shiab;
- the deformation-complex property;
- a healthy spin-3/2 constraint system;
- effective four-dimensional propagation;
- emergent chirality;
- exactly three observed generations;
- Einstein or Standard Model recovery;
- anomaly cancellation, unitarity or radiative closure.

## Current gate status

No fatal gate has yet been scored zero because Phase 1 has not found a contradiction in a fully fixed model. Nearly every hard gate remains at `1 — not demonstrated`. This is not a soft pass; it is a disciplined refusal to promote an incompletely reconstructed proposal into a tested theory.
