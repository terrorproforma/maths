# TP-16 — Gravity shielding and anti-gravity

**Author:** Angus Muffatti  
**Status:** QUEUED

## Goal

Prove the assumptions that forbid local gravitational shielding or reversed universal free fall in ordinary General Relativity, then test one minimal consistent evasion against all present constraints.

## Baseline no-go question

Under Einstein gravity, universal metric coupling, ordinary positive matter, and standard asymptotic/boundary conditions, can a finite local device shield the exterior gravitational monopole or reverse free fall?

## Candidate evasions

- scalar–tensor or vector–tensor gravity;
- torsion or nonmetricity;
- massive gravity;
- screening mechanisms;
- negative effective stress;
- nonlocal coupling;
- equivalence-principle violation.

## Required outputs

- exact no-go assumptions and proof;
- complete evading action;
- physical degree count and stability;
- Newtonian, PPN, binary, gravitational-wave, and compact-object limits;
- equivalence-principle and fifth-force constraints;
- invariant definition of shielding or anti-gravity;
- quantitative prediction or decisive obstruction.
