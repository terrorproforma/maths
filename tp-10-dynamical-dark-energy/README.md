# TP-10 — Dynamical dark energy under Bianchi/Lovelock constraints

**Author:** Angus Muffatti  
**Status:** QUEUED

## Goal

Classify exactly which assumptions must change if the effective dark-energy contribution evolves, then complete one stable model through current cosmological and local-gravity tests.

## Core consistency issue

A naive replacement \(\Lambda\to\Lambda(x)\) conflicts with covariant conservation because

\[
\nabla^\mu\left[\Lambda(x)g_{\mu\nu}\right]=\partial_\nu\Lambda.
\]

## Required outputs

- assumption-relaxation matrix: new field, new geometry, nonmetricity/torsion, higher dimensions, nonlocality, or modified conservation;
- complete action and field equations;
- ghost/gradient/hyperbolicity analysis;
- fifth-force and equivalence-principle tests;
- FLRW background and perturbations;
- CMB, BAO, SNe, growth, and local-gravity recovery;
- one dimensionless discriminator.
