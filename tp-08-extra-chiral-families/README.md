# TP-08 — Two additional chiral matter families

**Author:** Angus Muffatti  
**Status:** QUEUED

## Goal

Determine whether exactly two additional chiral families can follow from a structural condition rather than arbitrary replication, and whether any such spectrum is compatible with established particle physics and cosmology.

## Required outputs

- complete chiral representation content;
- local and global anomaly cancellation;
- proof of why the number of added families is exactly two;
- mass-generation mechanism without silently making the theory vectorlike;
- electroweak, Higgs, flavour, and collider predictions;
- cosmological abundance, decay, and stability analysis;
- treatment of mirror and unwanted light states.

## Terminal outcome

A viable anomaly-free model with a structural family-number result, or a sharp obstruction for the declared model class.
